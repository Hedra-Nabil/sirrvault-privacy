Sirrvault Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

Important: Sirrvault is a zero-knowledge password manager. We cannot access your passwords or master password.

Introduction

Sirrvault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address when you create an account.
  • Profile Data: Optional profile information.
  • Payment Information: Processed by Google Play billing.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type and OS version.
  • Usage Data: App features used and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics).

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your master password.
  • Your vault plain-text sensitive content.
  • Biometric data from your device sensor storage.

Data Security

  • Data is encrypted locally using strong encryption before sync.
  • Master password does not leave your device.
  • Encrypted vault data is stored in cloud infrastructure.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers and as required by law.

Your Rights

  • Access account data.
  • Export encrypted vault data.
  • Delete account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
