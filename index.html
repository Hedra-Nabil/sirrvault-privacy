Sirrvault Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

Important: Sirrvault is a zero-knowledge password manager. We cannot access your passwords or master password under any circumstances.

Introduction

Sirrvault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our password manager application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address when you create an account
  • Profile Data: Optional profile information
  • Payment Information: Processed securely by Google Play (we don't store payment details)

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • Usage Data: App features used, crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (via Firebase Analytics)

Information We DO NOT Collect

  • ❌ Your master password
  • ❌ Your actual passwords or sensitive data
  • ❌ Biometric data (stored only on your device)
  • ❌ Content of your vault entries

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Sirrvault service
  • Sync your encrypted vault across your devices
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Send important security notifications
  • Process premium subscriptions
  • Detect and prevent fraud

Data Security

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

  • Your data is encrypted locally using AES-256 encryption before being uploaded
  • Only you have the decryption keys (derived from your master password)
  • We cannot decrypt or access your vault contents
  • Your master password never leaves your device

Encryption Details

  • Algorithm: AES-256-CBC
  • Key Derivation: PBKDF2 with unique salt per entry
  • Storage: Encrypted data stored in Firebase Firestore
  • Local Storage: Encrypted using Hive with secure storage

Additional Security Measures

  • Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face unlock)
  • Secure enclave for encryption keys
  • Automatic session timeout
  • Protected screenshots in recent apps

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only in these circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google Cloud) for data storage and analytics
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger/acquisition (with notice to users)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • ✅ Access your account data
  • ✅ Export your vault data (encrypted)
  • ✅ Delete your account and all associated data
  • ✅ Opt-out of analytics collection
  • ✅ Withdraw consent at any time

Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Inactive Accounts: Data may be deleted after 2 years of inactivity

Children's Privacy

Sirrvault is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

International Data Transfers

Your encrypted data may be stored on servers located outside your country. All data remains encrypted regardless of storage location.
